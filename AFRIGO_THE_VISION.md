## THE PROBLEM YOU'RE SOLVING

### Today's Reality (The Broken System)

A farmer in Uganda grows premium cocoa beans. Here's what happens:

```
FARMER (Uganda):
  Grows cocoa → Gets paid $1.50/kg
  
MIDDLEMAN 1:
  Buys from farmer → Adds $0.30/kg (20% markup) → Sells to middleman 2
  
MIDDLEMAN 2:
  Buys → Adds $0.30/kg (20% markup) → Sells to middleman 3
  
MIDDLEMAN 3:
  Buys → Adds $0.30/kg (20% markup) → Sells to exporter
  
EXPORTER:
  Buys → Adds $0.20/kg (13% markup) → Ships to London
  
IMPORTER (London):
  Buys at $2.60/kg
  
RETAILER:
  Sells to consumer at $5.00/kg

CONSUMER (London):
  Pays $5.00/kg for cocoa

RESULT:
  ❌ Farmer earned $1.50 = 30% of retail price
  ❌ Middlemen collectively took 40% of price

  ❌ Nobody knows if quality is verified
  ❌ If product is bad, everyone blames everyone
```
