## TIER 1 COUNTRIES (Launch Priority)

### KENYA
**Capital:** Nairobi  
**Population:** 54 million  
**Language:** English, Swahili

#### 1.1  Money Transmission & Payment
**License Required:** YES - Remittance Provider License from Central Bank of Kenya (CBK)

**Requirements:**
- Minimum paid-up capital: KES 40 million ($300,000 USD)
- Shareholders with >20% stake: Full due diligence
- Directors: Clean record, no financial crimes
- Auditor: Big 4 recommended (KPMG, Deloitte, PWC, EY)
- Compliance Officer: Dedicated, not part-time
- Risk Management Policy: Detailed documentation required

**Timeline:** 6-8 months  
**Cost:** ~$50,000 (legal, fees, setup)

**Status:** 🟢 **STARTED** - License application submitted January 2026, pending CBK review

#### 1.2 KYC/AML Requirements
**Framework:** Central Bank's KYC Guidelines 2024

**Individual Customers:**
- Full name + at least one ID type
- Proof of address (utility bill, lease agreement)
- Source of funds verification
- Beneficial ownership disclosure (if company)

**High-Risk Customers** (>$5,000 transaction):
- Enhanced due diligence
- Video KYC (face-to-face verification)
- Sanctions list screening (UN, EU, US)
- Source of wealth documentation

**Ongoing Monitoring:**
- Transaction reporting system (monthly)
- Suspicious activity reports (within 24 hours)
- Annual customer review & re-verification

#### 1.3 Agricultural Trade Regulations
**Primary Regulator:** Kenya Plant & Produce Inspection Service (KEPHIS)

**Export Requirements:**
- Phytosanitary certificate (KEPHIS-issued) - 3-day processing
- Certificate of Origin (Chamber of Commerce) - 1 day
- Export permit (for restricted items)
- Quality certificate (for commodity exports)

**Compliance Check:** Products must be:
- Not from demarcated disease areas
- Treated per export standards if required
- Properly packaged & labeled
- Accompanied by test certificates

**Cost:** 500-1,500 KES per shipment depending on product

#### 1.4 Tax Obligations
**Regulator:** Kenya Revenue Authority (KRA)

**What We Must Do:**
- Register as VAT supplier (if turnover >KES 12 million)
- File monthly VAT returns
- Maintain transaction records (6 years)
- Issue tax invoices to business customers
- Provide annual tax statements to traders

**Trading Fee Tax:**
- 2-3% platform fee is VAT-able
- We collect 16% VAT on our fees
- Must deposit to KRA monthly

**Trader Taxes:**
- Self-employed traders: File personal income tax
- Companies: File corporate tax
- We provide monthly transaction summaries for tax reporting

#### 1.5 Consumer Protection
**Framework:** Consumer Protection Act 2012

**Our Protections:**
- Clear terms of service (in English & Swahili)
- Transparent pricing display
- Dispute resolution mechanism (within 30 days)
- Refund policy posted
- Contact info for complaints

### UGANDA
**Capital:** Kampala  
**Population:** 48 million  
**Language:** English, Luganda

#### 2.1 Money Transmission
**License:** YES - Remittance Provider License from Bank of Uganda (BoU)

**Requirements:**
- Minimum capital: UGX 10 billion ($2.7 million USD)
- Local representative (Ugandan citizen/company)
- Risk management framework
- Compliance officer
- Annual audit by Big 4 firm

**Timeline:** 6-9 months  
**Cost:** ~$60,000

**Status:** 🟡 **Pending** - Initial consultation with BoU scheduled May 2026

#### 2.2 KYC/AML
**Framework:** Anti-Money Laundering Act 2013, AML Regulations 2019

**Customer Identification:**
- Name, address, identification
- Source of funds verification
- Beneficial ownership (corporate customers)
- Video KYC for high-value (>$10,000)

**Ongoing Compliance:**
- Transaction monitoring (automated rules)
- Suspicious activity reporting (24-48 hours)
- Customer risk rating (monthly review)
- Sanctions screening (continuous)

#### 2.3 Agricultural
**Regulator:** Ministry of Agriculture, Animal Industry & Fisheries (MAAIF)
- Export permit for agricultural products
- Phytosanitary certificate (3-5 days processing)
- Quality certificates for specific products

#### 2.4 Tax
**Regulator:** Uganda Revenue Authority (URA)

**Requirements:**
- Tax identification number (TIN)
- Monthly VAT returns (if applicable)
- Annual income tax filing
- Maintain 5-year audit trail

## TRADING REGULATIONS & RESTRICTIONS

### Prohibited Products (All Countries)
❌ Weapons, ammunition, explosives  
❌ Drugs, narcotics, controlled substances  
❌ Counterfeit goods, plagiarized products  
❌ Endangered species or products  
❌ Stolen property  
❌ Child labor products  

### Restricted Products (Varying by Country)
⚠️ Alcohol (license required in most countries)  
⚠️ Pesticides (agricultural chemicals - regulated)  
⚠️ Minerals (diamonds, gold - special licensing)  
⚠️ Cultural artifacts (export permit required)
