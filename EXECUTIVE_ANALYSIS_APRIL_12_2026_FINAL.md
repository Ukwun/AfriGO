# WHAT AFRIGO ACTUALLY IS

## The Mission
**Build the Pan-African Digital Marketplace** that eliminates middlemen and transforms agricultural commodity trading by directly connecting farmers, buyers, and exporters.

## The Vision in One Sentence
*"Imagine a farmer in rural Uganda selling directly to a boutique chocolate maker in London, knowing exactly what they're paying, without 5 middlemen taking cuts, with complete transparency about the product, and getting paid securely the same day."*

## How It Works (The User Journey)

### **For Farmers/Suppliers:**
```
1. Create Account → Verify Identity (KYC)
2. List Product → Upload photos, specify quality, set availability
3. Receive Offers → Buyers send RFQs, suppliers bid
4. Negotiate → Agree on price, terms, delivery date
5. Sign Contract → Both parties e-sign agreement
6. Ship Product → GPS tracking, real-time updates
7. Get Paid → Money released automatically on delivery confirmation
```

### **For Buyers/Importers:**
```
1. Create Account → Verify Identity
2. Search Products → Browse available lots by region, quality, price
3. Create RFQ → Specify what you need, quantity, delivery location
4. Receive Bids → Multiple suppliers respond with quotes
5. Compare & Choose → See quality history, supplier ratings, prices
6. Negotiate Terms → Finalize contract with winner
7. Track Shipment → Real-time GPS, temperature monitoring
8. Receive & Verify → Accept delivery, confirm quality
9. Pay Securely → Money released from escrow to supplier
```

### **Platform Benefits:**
- **Farmers:** Sell directly, keep 97-98% of sale price (vs losing 40-60% to middlemen)
- **Buyers:** Get authentic products, transparent pricing, quality verification
- **Platform:** 2-3% transaction fee on every trade
- **Society:** Better traceability, food safety, reduced counterfeits, more jobs

# THE CORE PROBLEMS WE'RE SOLVING

## Problem 1: Inefficient Supply Chain
**Current Reality:**
- Farmer grows cocoa in Uganda
- Middleman #1 buys at $1.50/kg
- Middleman #2 transports, buys at $1.80/kg
- Middleman #3 aggregates, sells at $2.10/kg
- Exporter bottles/commodities, sells at $2.40/kg
- Importer/Distributor sells at $2.70/kg
- Retailer sells to consumer at $5.00/kg
- **Farmer makes $1.50, Consumer pays $5.00 = 70% goes to middlemen**

**AfriGo Solution:**
- Farmer grows cocoa in Uganda
- Farmer lists on AfriGo at $2.50/kg
- Buyer in London buys directly at $2.60/kg (saves money, farmer gets more)
- **Farmer makes $2.50 (+67%), Buyer pays $2.60 (saves 48%), No middlemen**

---

## Problem 2: Zero Transparency
**Current Reality:**
- Buyer in London doesn't know if cocoa came from Uganda, Ghana, or Cameroon
- Doesn't know if product is authentic or counterfeit
- Doesn't know harvest date, quality tests, or handling conditions
- Assumes product is "fine" based on middleman's word

**AfriGo Solution:**
- Every product has immutable history: farm location → harvest date → quality tests → shipping → delivery
- Buyers see labs tests (moisture %, fermentation %, defects)
- Buyers see GPS tracking during shipment
- Buyers see delivery photos & timestamps
- **Complete chain of custody verified**

---

## Problem 3: Quality Disputes
**Current Reality:**
- Buyer receives product, claims it's lower quality than promised
- Seller says it's fine, argues about value
- No data to prove either way
- Money dispute, relationship destroyed

**AfriGo Solution:**
- Lab tests happen before shipping (buyer sees reports)
- AI analyzes product photos (detects defects, mold, color issues)
- Delivery includes quality verification photos
- If quality dispute: Evidence already documented
- **Disputes resolved by data, not arguments**

---

## Problem 4: Payment Risk
**Current Reality:**
- Seller ships product hoping buyer will pay
- Buyer pays hoping product will arrive in good condition
- Both have risk, both lose trust

**AfriGo Solution:**
- Buyer pays AfriGo (not seller) → money held in escrow
- Seller ships with confidence (payment guaranteed)
- Buyer receives product & verifies quality
- Payment released automatically from escrow to seller
- If dispute: Evidence reviewed, money goes to rightful party
- **Nobody loses money, everyone has security**

---

## Problem 5: Counterfeits & Fraud
**Current Reality:**
- No way to verify if product is real or fake
- No full traceability (could be reused packaging)
- Forged quality certificates easy to create

**AfriGo Solution:**
- Every lot has cryptographic QR code (can't be faked)
- Immutable event log (every action timestamped & signed)
- Lab reports blockchain-verified (Phase 2)
- Behavioral analysis flags suspicious traders
- Trust scoring prevents low-trust users from large deals
- **Fraud detected & prevented before money moves**

# THE INTELLIGENCE LAYER

## What Makes AfriGo "Smart" (Weeks 9-13)

This is what transforms AfriGo from a "listing site" into a platform that **knows its users** and **prevents problems before they happen**.

## 1. Trust Scoring (Know Your User)

Every user gets a score (0-100) that determines what they can do on the platform.

**How It's Calculated:**
```
Trust Score = Base (40) + History (30) + Behavior (20) + Penalties (-∞)

BASE: Everyone starts with 40/100 (everyone deserves a chance)

TRANSACTION HISTORY (+30 max):
├── 1 completed trade: +2 points
├── 10 completed trades: +20 points
├── 50 completed trades: +30 points (capped)
├── On-time payment: +1 per trade
└── Early payment: +2 per trade

BEHAVIOR BONUS (+20 max):
├── Email verified: +3 points
├── Phone verified: +3 points
├── Complete profile (photo, bio, location): +2
├── No disputes filed: +2
├── Response time <2hrs: +2
└── KYC verified (national ID): +8

PENALTIES (can be severe):
├── Late payment: -5 points per
├── Failed delivery: -3 points per
├── Dispute filed (even if won): -2
├── Dispute lost: -5 points
├── Chargeback: -10 points
├── Scam reported: -50 points (BANNED)
```

**Real World Example:**
```
Farmer Ali - Trust Score Evolution

Day 1 (Account Created):
   Base: 40
   + Verified email: 3
   + Verified phone: 3
   + Complete profile: 2
   + KYC verified: 8
   = 56/100 (⭐⭐⭐⭐☆ Good start)

After 10 successful trades (3 months):
   + 20 points (trade history)
   + 10 points (on-time payments)
   = 96/100 (⭐⭐⭐⭐⭐ Excellent)

Impact: Ali can now:
├── Auto-approved for deals <$5,000
├── Access credit line (buy first, pay later)
├── Priority matching with international buyers
├── Higher fees waived (encouragement)
└── Appears higher in search results
```

**System Benefits:**
- High-trust users get preferential treatment
- Low-trust users monitored closely
- Frequent traders rewarded
- Bad actors isolated or banned

## 2. Fraud Detection (Stop Bad Actors)

**Real-time risk scoring for every transaction:**

```
Transaction: Buyer Janet buying $150,000 cocoa (unusual size)

Risk Calculation:

USER RISK (&nbsp;Janet's score: 45/100 = concerning):
   +30 points

TRANSACTION PATTERN:
   • Janet usually buys: 2 tons
   • This buy: 50 tons (25x larger)
   • +20 points
   
   • Janet usually spends: $5,000
   • This buy: $150,000 (30x larger)
   • +20 points
   
   • New supplier (never traded before)
   • +10 points
   = +50 points

PAYMENT METHOD:
   • New payment method (hasn't used before)
   • +15 points
   
   • Country with higher fraud rates
   • +10 points
   = +25 points

FINAL RISK: 30 + 50 + 25 = 105/100 (capped 100)

⚠️🚨 HIGH RISK ACTION:
✓ Require additional verification (2FA + SMS)
✓ Manual review by compliance officer
✓ Transaction held 24h for monitoring
✓ Message to buyer: "Please verify unusual purchase size"

If Janet confirms (verifies her intent):
   → Funds released but closely monitored
   → If delivery succeeds: Trust increases
   → If dispute filed: Both flagged for fraud investigation
```

**System Prevents:**
- Fake accounts doing transactions
- Hacked accounts being used fraudulently
- Stolen payment methods
- Money laundering patterns
- Chargeback fraud
